## Recommended H-scope decision

**Choose the existing ordinary-nonmetal site-total target `T`, including graph H, as the one current field-14 convention for ordinary nonmetal chemical counts. Normalize new RDKit/Open Babel imports to `T = E + R`; derive residual non-graph H as `R = max(T - E, 0)` at consuming boundaries.** Here `E` is the number of graph-H neighbours and `R` includes unmaterialized bracket H. Keep graph membership authoritative in the existing topology and keep exact imported counts in immutable source history. Do not retain a second mutable residual/total table, introduce an encoding mode, or add a DTO or public residual-H property.

This selects the smaller of the two normalization routes already proposed in [Stage5 §5](/home/zhangzhiyuan/hotpot/plan/20_fact_rule_architecture/stages/stage05/hydrogen-defaults/decisions.md:212). Literal residual storage would also change the approved inferred field-14 convention, including fully explicit ordinary molecules; it is not recommended. Retain the approved inferred metal raw-zero and real-H/H2 exceptions. They are scoped legacy encodings, not universal physical inventories: their chemical inventories require their graph and current declared-H facts, rather than treating raw field 14 as a total.

The public integer property/setter, scalar codec, omitted-input display and strict readiness boundary stay in place. A supplied ordinary-nonmetal value is an exact target declaration; default inference cannot rewrite it. An explicit target below `E` does not permit a projection to erase graph H or invent a negative residual. Intentional removal/protonation and explicit-number operations retain separately reviewed behavior. Characterized nonchemical float64 values remain preserved at the permissive numeric boundary; this decision does not add argument validation or new scalar exceptions.
